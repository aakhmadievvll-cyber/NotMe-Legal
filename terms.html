1. What the app is

Not Me! is a free party game for one iPhone or iPad. Everyone plays around a single device and passes it from hand to hand. Version 1.1 has 22 game modes, 121 bundled packs and 10,189 cards. The app is fully bilingual: the interface and the card content are available in Russian and in English. It needs iOS 26.0 or later and runs on iPhone and iPad.

The app contains no network code. It runs completely in Airplane Mode. There is no server, no backend, no cloud sync, no account, no login and no registration. There are no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party SDK of any kind. There are no in-app purchases, no subscriptions and no virtual currency. Nothing inside the app costs money.

The app collects nothing about you. Its App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected". What the app stores on your own device, and why none of it leaves the device, is described in privacy.html.

  • The developer never sees this content and cannot review, moderate, recover or restore it.
  • You can delete your own packs inside the app: Settings has a Delete my packs button with a confirmation step, shown once you have created at least one pack.

6. The 18+ packs are opt-in, and the host decides who plays

Some packs are marked 18+: flirting, hookups, nightlife, references to alcohol, and dark lateral-thinking riddles. They are hidden by default. They appear only after someone turns on the "show 18+ packs" setting.

That toggle is a convenience content filter, not a parental control. There is no passcode on it, no age verification behind it, and nothing in the app checks who is holding the phone. Anyone with the device in their hands can turn it on or off.

These packs are intended for adults. If you are the host — the person whose phone is on the table — you decide which packs are open and who is sitting in the circle. Check the group before you turn 18+ packs on, and turn them back off before handing the phone to a younger group. If you are playing with minors, or with people who have not agreed to adult content, keep the toggle off and pick a family-friendly pack.

7. Dares and prompts are entertainment

The cards are written to make a room laugh and argue. They are entertainment, not instructions and not advice.

  • Every card is optional. A player can skip any card, pass their turn, or leave the game at any moment, for any reason or none. Skipping is a normal part of playing, not a forfeit you must justify.
  • Do not do anything unsafe, illegal, degrading or harmful because a card mentions it. A card is a suggestion on a screen; what actually happens in the room is a choice the players make themselves.
  • Respect consent. Do not pressure anyone into answering a question or doing a dare, and stop when someone says stop.
  • One mode, "Who am I?" («Кто я?»), asks a player to hold the phone to their forehead and tilt it to answer. Hold the device firmly and give yourself room. The app reads the tilt live through the device motion sensor; the readings are never stored and never sent anywhere.
  • You are responsible for your own actions and for what your group does while playing.

8. Alcohol

Nothing in the app requires anyone to drink. Some 18+ cards mention drinks or nightlife; any of them can be skipped like any other card. The app records nothing about a game, so nothing anyone drinks, or declines to drink, is counted or kept. Drinking non-alcoholic, or not drinking at all, is always a valid way to play. Follow the drinking-age law where you are, and never drive after drinking.
