Game Night: Group Party Games — Legal & Support

Public legal and support pages for the iOS party game Game Night: Group Party Games («Посиделки»); until version 1.4 the same app was called Not Me! («Не Я!»). Free games that work offline. No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Optional premium packs, AI themes and AI questions for the quiz games with the Посиделки Плюс / Game Night Plus subscription.

About the app

  • App name: Game Night: Group Party Games (Russian: «Посиделки: игры для компании»)
  • Platform: iOS 26.0 or later, iPhone and iPad
  • Bundle ID: com.alba.SpyBest · Apple ID 6762642879
  • Price: free to download; every game is free, with 15 bundled packs (one for every kind of game) always free. Optional auto-renewable subscription Game Night Plus for premium packs, AI themes and AI questions. No ads.
  • 31 game modes (three of them quiz games whose built-in packs are built on public data), 179 content packs, 13,328 cards, Russian and English
  • Developer: Akhmadiev Albert IE (Republic of Armenia)
