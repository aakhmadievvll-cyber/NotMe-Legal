Support

Support for Game Night: Group Party Games («Посиделки», until version 1.4 Not Me! / «Не Я!») on iPhone and iPad. Effective date: October 18, 2026.

The games are free and work on one phone passed around the table, completely offline. Everything they play with — 31 game modes and 179 packs — is bundled inside the app. 15 packs, one for every kind of game, are free for good, so every game can be played without paying; the other packs come with the Game Night Plus subscription, which also includes the one online feature, creating with AI — card themes and questions for the quiz games (see below). If something is broken or confusing, write to us through one of the channels below.

Try this first

Most reports are solved by one of these steps.

  • Update to the latest version. Open the App Store, tap your profile picture, and check the list of available updates. The current version is 1.7. The app needs iOS 26.0 or later.
  • Close and reopen the app. Swipe it away from the app switcher and start it again. If that does not help, restart the device.
  • Games never need the internet. The games do not connect to any server, have no account and no login, and play exactly the same in Airplane Mode. Wi-Fi, mobile data, VPNs and captive portals cannot cause a bug in a game. Only creating AI themes and questions needs a connection — see "AI themes and questions" below.
  • Missing packs? Check the 18+ filter. Packs marked 18+ are hidden by default. Turn them on with the Show 18+ packs switch in Settings. This is a content filter for convenience, not a parental control: there is no passcode and no age check. The App Store rating for the app is 16+. A pack with a lock is a premium pack — see the next section.
  • Putting settings back. The settings are separate switches, so set each one the way you want it: Reveal (hold or tap to reveal a card), Haptics, Night table (the dark look for playing in a dim room) and Show 18+ packs. Deleting the app and installing it again clears all of them, but it also deletes the packs you made yourself and everything you made with AI, so keep that as a last resort.
  • Phone vibrating too much? Turn off Haptics in Settings.
  • Tilt not registering in "Who am I?" That mode is the only one that reads device motion, and it reads it live while you hold the phone to your forehead. Nothing from the motion sensor is saved or sent anywhere. If the tilt is not picked up, hold the phone steady between answers and tilt in one clear movement.
  • Your own packs, things made with AI and reinstalling. Packs you create and AI themes and questions are stored only on your device. They are not backed up to us, not synced, and there is no share, export or upload path for them. You can remove them yourself in Settings, with a confirmation step. If you delete the app, they are deleted with it, and reinstalling gives you a fresh app with only the built-in packs. We cannot restore them, because we do not keep a copy.

Quiz games: Guesstimate, Rarest Answer, Top Answers

  • Where do the numbers come from? In the built-in packs, every Guesstimate number comes from a named public source — the UN, NASA, the FAO, Guinness World Records, scientific papers, company reports, Wikipedia and others — and the answer shows it, with the year when the number changes over time. Some figures are rounded. Questions made by AI have no source: the number is the AI's estimate, and the answer says so: "AI estimate, unverified".
  • How is an answer's popularity counted? In the built-in packs for Rarest Answer and Top Answers it comes from a year of Wikipedia page views of each answer's article; for everyday categories (fruits, dishes and the like) it is an editors' estimate, and the board says which. It is not a poll, and what you type does not change it. In lists made by AI, both the list and the popularity are the AI's estimate, as the label "List and popularity: AI estimate" says.
  • "Not on the AI's list". A list made by AI can be incomplete. If your answer in Rarest Answer is not on such a list, the game says "Not on the AI's list — it may be incomplete": it scores nothing, but it does not cost you a life either.
  • The full list. Settings → Data sources lists every source and dataset of the built-in packs with its licence; each quiz's rules link to it too. What you type in the quiz games is checked on your device and never sent anywhere, and your records stay on the device. If a number in a built-in pack looks out of date or a right answer was not accepted, email us the question and what you typed.

Game Night Plus and premium packs

  • What is free. All 31 games are free. 15 packs, one for every kind of game, are free for good, and every day one more pack of each kind is free too — it is marked "Free today". Packs you make yourself and AI themes and questions you have already created are always free. The other packs, and creating AI themes and questions after your free ones, come with Game Night Plus: Plus Monthly or Plus Yearly, which unlock exactly the same things. In the US they cost $3.00 a month or $10.00 a year; elsewhere the App Store shows the price in your currency before you buy.
  • A pack with a "Plus" sticker and a lock. It is a premium pack. Tap it to see sample cards and the games it works in; "Unlock with Game Night Plus" opens the subscription screen. Once you subscribe, the locks disappear everywhere.
  • Packs do not need the internet. The app decides on your device, from your App Store subscription, which packs are open; it does not ask our server, and opening the subscription screen from a pack does not contact our server either. If your phone is offline when the subscription renews, premium packs stay open for up to 3 days after the end date the App Store last reported.
  • Russian App Store. In the Russian storefront every pack bundled in the app is free, because App Store purchases are not available there. AI themes and questions still need Game Night Plus there, apart from the one free theme and the one free set of questions.
  • Restoring a subscription. On a new device or after reinstalling, tap "Restore purchases" on the Game Night Plus screen or in Settings. If premium packs are locked although you are subscribed, or the app says "We couldn't confirm your subscription", connect to the internet, tap "Restore purchases", and check that the subscription is active in Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  • Cancelling or changing plan. In the app, tap "Manage or change plan" in the Game Night Plus section of Settings; or open the iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Game Night Plus. Cancelling stops the next renewal; Plus works until the end of the paid period, and premium packs lock again at the latest 3 days after it ends. Deleting the app does not cancel it. AI themes and questions you already made stay on your device.
  • Refunds. Payments are processed by Apple, and only Apple can issue a refund: reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Game Night Plus, AI themes and AI questions are not available in mainland China.

AI themes and questions

  • What it is. Describe a topic and, in about half a minute, the app builds a theme for it — words with taboo words, locations with roles and word pairs — playable in up to 17 games. Each Apple Account can create one theme for free. After that, AI themes need Game Night Plus (see above).
  • AI questions. Since version 1.7 the AI also writes quiz questions on your topic: questions with numbers for Guesstimate, or answer lists for Top Answers and Rarest Answer — in about a minute. Their numbers, lists and popularity are the AI's estimate: we do not check them, and the game labels them that way. Each Apple Account can create one set of questions for free — separately from the free theme; after that, it takes Game Night Plus. The questions you create appear in the pack choice of those games, in the language they were made in.
  • Where to find it. The sticker on the Play screen and in the "Made with AI" section in Packs, the "Create with AI" tile when you choose a pack, the "Want more questions?" sticker when you choose packs in the quiz games (subscribers get an "AI questions" or "AI lists" tile there), the "Play it on your own topic?" sticker after a game (never after the very first one, and at most once an evening), or the Game Night Plus section in Settings. When you open the studio from the Play screen or Packs, you can choose what to create: card themes, Guesstimate questions, Top Answers lists or Rarest Answer lists. The Play-screen sticker reads "Your own AI theme" while your free theme is available, "New: AI questions for the quiz games" when only the free set of questions is left, "Game Night Plus" after that, and "Create a theme or questions with AI" once you subscribe. If the Play screen shows no sticker, use one of the other entries.
  • You can close the studio. A theme or a set of questions keeps being created after you close the screen. When it is ready it appears in the "Made with AI" section in Packs and in the pack lists of the games it fits.
  • "Sign in to the App Store". The free theme and the free set of questions are tied to your Apple Account, so the device must be signed in to the App Store (Settings → your name → Media & Purchases). Sign in, then tap the button in the studio again.
  • "That topic is a bit narrow…" or "The AI couldn't turn this topic into a theme" (or "into questions"). The topic was too narrow to fill a theme or questions, or it asked for something the content rules do not allow — sexual content, hate, real private people, instructions for harm. For questions, countries and capitals, politics, religion, wars, disease and alcohol are not available either. If the app says Rarest Answer needs complete lists, pick a topic whose members can all be listed: planets, zodiac signs, the colours of the rainbow. Rephrase it. A refused request does not create your free theme or questions, but it uses one of their 3 attempts (see the next point).
  • "Your free try has been used". Each Apple Account gets one free theme and one free set of questions, with up to 3 attempts at each. A refused request, or one that failed after it was passed to the AI, uses an attempt; a request that failed before reaching the AI does not. Once the free theme (or set of questions) is created or the 3 attempts are used, new themes (or questions) need Game Night Plus. If your free theme or questions were created but never arrived — for example, the connection dropped — open the app again within 72 hours: it finishes them by itself, or tap "Finish that theme" in the studio.
  • "Free themes (or questions) are paused", "temporarily unavailable" or "AI questions aren't available yet". The service is resting for a while — because of demand, a monthly limit or maintenance; AI questions can be unavailable even while themes work. Try again later; the app says when if it knows. Your games are not affected.
  • "Plus limit" or "Too many attempts in a row". Game Night Plus allows up to 10 generations a day and 30 in any 30 days, themes and questions together; a day starts at midnight UTC, and refused requests count too. The app tells you when you can create new themes and questions. "Too many attempts in a row" means too many requests came from your internet address in a short time: take a short break and try again.
  • Something wrong in a theme or questions you made? Open it in the "Made with AI" section in Packs and tap "Report" — it opens this page; the link itself sends nothing. Tell us by e-mail what the theme was about, what the card or question said and what is wrong with it.

How to reach us

  • Email: InnoAppDesign@yandex.com — best for anything involving your name, your email, purchases, or details you do not want public.
  • Support form: https://forms.gle/f4DUfvcd97iUuqW68
  • Bug reports and feature requests: GitHub issues

All three are outside the app. The app has no contact button and no feedback screen, so write to us from your mail app or your browser. Whatever you type into an email, the form or a GitHub issue is what leaves your device; the form and GitHub are websites run by other companies, and the app sends us nothing about your message.

Nothing is attached automatically either. The app has no crash reporting and no diagnostics, so we only know what you describe in your message.

Write in English or Russian. Both are fine. The app is published by Akhmadiev Albert IE (individual entrepreneur Albert Akhmadiev), Republic of Armenia.

What to put in a report

The more of this you include, the faster we can reproduce the problem.

  1. Device model, for example iPhone 15 Pro or iPad Air.
  2. iOS version, from Settings > General > About on the device.
  3. App version. The current release is 1.7; the app's page in the App Store shows the version you have.
  4. App language you were playing in, Russian or English.
  5. Game mode and pack you were using.
  6. For AI themes and questions and premium packs: roughly when you tried, the message the app showed, and whether you have Game Night Plus or were using the free theme or the free set of questions.
  7. Steps to reproduce, in order, from opening the app to the moment it went wrong.
  8. What you expected to happen, and what happened instead.
  9. A screenshot or short screen recording, if it makes the problem clearer. Attach it to an email rather than posting it publicly.

Never send us your Apple Account password or payment card details. We will never ask for them.

Please keep private information out of GitHub

GitHub issues are public. Anyone can read them, and they stay readable after the issue is closed. Do not post your email address, phone number, full name, home address, screenshots of other apps, purchase receipts, or the contents of personal packs you wrote with your friends' names in them. If your report needs any of that, send it by email instead.

Privacy requests

The games collect nothing: no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no third-party SDK of any kind. Your settings, the last game modes you played, the number of games started, the player names from your last game, your best scores, your own packs and everything you made with AI live on your device. Checking which packs are unlocked happens on your device too and sends nothing to us.

Only creating with AI sends data: your description of a theme or questions goes to our server and to the AI provider to create what you asked for, together with Apple's signed purchase records and your IP address. Our server keeps keyed hashes of Apple transaction identifiers (including separate records for the free theme and the free set of questions), a record of each recent generation for the fair-use limits (30 days), the finished theme or question set for 72 hours, and short technical logs without your description. That is why the App Store privacy section, since version 1.2, lists Other User Content, Purchase History, User ID and Product Interaction, all used only for app functionality and none for tracking. The full details are in the Privacy Policy.

To ask what we hold or to ask for deletion, email us. Our server does not know your name or email address, so tell us what you need and we will explain what we can find and delete. To erase everything on your device, delete the app. The rules for using the app and the subscription are in the Terms of Use.
