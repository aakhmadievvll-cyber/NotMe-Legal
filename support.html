Support

Support for Not Me! Offline Party Games («Не Я!») on iPhone and iPad. Effective date: September 14, 2026.

The app is free, works on one phone passed around the table, and runs completely offline. Everything it plays with — 22 game modes and 121 packs — is bundled inside the app. If something is broken or confusing, write to us through one of the channels below.

Try this first

Most reports are solved by one of these steps.

  • Update to the latest version. Open the App Store, tap your profile picture, and check the list of available updates. The current version is 1.1. The app needs iOS 26.0 or later.
  • Close and reopen the app. Swipe it away from the app switcher and start it again. If that does not help, restart the device.
  • Do not look for a network problem. The game has no network code at all. It does not connect to any server, it has no account and no login, and it plays exactly the same in Airplane Mode. Wi-Fi, mobile data, VPNs and captive portals cannot cause a bug here.
  • Missing packs? Check the 18+ filter. Packs marked 18+ are hidden by default. Turn them on with the Show 18+ packs switch in Settings. This is a content filter for convenience, not a parental control: there is no passcode and no age check. The App Store rating for the app is 16+.
  • Putting settings back. The settings are separate switches, so set each one the way you want it: Reveal (hold or tap to reveal a card), Haptics, Night table (the dark look for playing in a dim room) and Show 18+ packs. Deleting the app and installing it again clears all of them, but it also deletes the packs you made yourself, so keep that as a last resort.
  • Phone vibrating too much? Turn off Haptics in Settings.
  • Tilt not registering in "Who am I?" That mode is the only one that reads device motion, and it reads it live while you hold the phone to your forehead. Nothing from the motion sensor is saved or sent anywhere. If the tilt is not picked up, hold the phone steady between answers and tilt in one clear movement.
  • Your own packs and reinstalling. Packs you create are stored only on your device. They are not backed up to us, not synced, and there is no share, export or upload path for them. You can remove them yourself with the Delete my packs button in Settings, which appears once you have made at least one. If you delete the app, your packs are deleted with it, and reinstalling gives you a fresh app with only the built-in packs. We cannot restore them, because we never had a copy.

How to reach us

  • Email: InnoAppDesign@yandex.com — best for anything involving your name, your email, or details you do not want public.
  • Support form: https://forms.gle/f4DUfvcd97iUuqW68
  • Bug reports and feature requests: GitHub issues

All three are outside the app. The app has no contact button, no feedback screen, and no way to open a link or an email for you, so write to us from your mail app or your browser. Whatever you type into an email, the form or a GitHub issue is what leaves your device; the form and GitHub are websites run by other companies, and the game itself sends nothing.

Nothing is attached automatically either. The app has no crash reporting and no diagnostics, so we only know what you describe in your message.

Write in English or Russian. Both are fine. The app is published by ИП Ахмадиев Альберт Маратович (individual entrepreneur Albert Maratovich Akhmadiev), Russia.

What to put in a report

The more of this you include, the faster we can reproduce the problem.

  1. Device model, for example iPhone 15 Pro or iPad Air.
  2. iOS version, from Settings > General > About on the device.
  3. App version. The current release is 1.1; the app's page in the App Store shows the version you have.
  4. App language you were playing in, Russian or English.
  5. Game mode and pack you were using.
  6. Steps to reproduce, in order, from opening the app to the moment it went wrong.
  7. What you expected to happen, and what happened instead.
  8. A screenshot or short screen recording, if it makes the problem clearer. Attach it to an email rather than posting it publicly.

Please keep private information out of GitHub

GitHub issues are public. Anyone can read them, and they stay readable after the issue is closed. Do not post your email address, phone number, full name, home address, screenshots of other apps, or the contents of personal packs you wrote with your friends' names in them. If your report needs any of that, send it by email instead.

Privacy requests

The app collects nothing. There is no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no third-party SDK of any kind. In the App Store the app is declared as Data Not Collected, and its privacy manifest declares no tracking and no collected data types.

Everything the app remembers lives on your device: your settings, the last game modes you played, and the packs you created yourself. There is no account and no server, so we cannot see your data, identify you, export your data, or delete it remotely. There is nothing on our side to delete.

To erase everything, delete the app from your device. That removes all local data, including your own packs. If you have a question about any of this, email us or read the full Privacy Policy. The rules for using the app are in the Terms of Use.
