Support

Support for Not Me! Offline Party Games («Не Я!») on iPhone and iPad. Effective date: September 27, 2026.

The games are free and work on one phone passed around the table, completely offline. Everything they play with — 22 game modes and 121 packs — is bundled inside the app. The one online feature is optional: AI themes with the Not Me! Plus subscription (see below). If something is broken or confusing, write to us through one of the channels below.

Try this first

Most reports are solved by one of these steps.

  • Update to the latest version. Open the App Store, tap your profile picture, and check the list of available updates. The current version is 1.2. The app needs iOS 26.0 or later.
  • Close and reopen the app. Swipe it away from the app switcher and start it again. If that does not help, restart the device.
  • Games never need the internet. The games do not connect to any server, have no account and no login, and play exactly the same in Airplane Mode. Wi-Fi, mobile data, VPNs and captive portals cannot cause a bug in a game. Only creating an AI theme needs a connection — see the next section.
  • Missing packs? Check the 18+ filter. Packs marked 18+ are hidden by default. Turn them on with the Show 18+ packs switch in Settings. This is a content filter for convenience, not a parental control: there is no passcode and no age check. The App Store rating for the app is 16+.
  • Putting settings back. The settings are separate switches, so set each one the way you want it: Reveal (hold or tap to reveal a card), Haptics, Night table (the dark look for playing in a dim room) and Show 18+ packs. Deleting the app and installing it again clears all of them, but it also deletes the packs you made yourself and your AI themes, so keep that as a last resort.
  • Phone vibrating too much? Turn off Haptics in Settings.
  • Tilt not registering in "Who am I?" That mode is the only one that reads device motion, and it reads it live while you hold the phone to your forehead. Nothing from the motion sensor is saved or sent anywhere. If the tilt is not picked up, hold the phone steady between answers and tilt in one clear movement.
  • Your own packs, AI themes and reinstalling. Packs you create and AI themes are stored only on your device. They are not backed up to us, not synced, and there is no share, export or upload path for them. You can remove them yourself in Settings, with a confirmation step. If you delete the app, they are deleted with it, and reinstalling gives you a fresh app with only the built-in packs. We cannot restore them, because we do not keep a copy.

AI themes and Not Me! Plus

  • What it is. Describe a topic and, in about half a minute, the app builds a theme for it — words with taboo words, locations with roles and word pairs — playable in up to 14 games. Each Apple Account can create one theme for free. After that, AI themes need Not Me! Plus: Plus Monthly or Plus Yearly, which unlock exactly the same thing. In the US they cost $3.00 a month or $10.00 a year; elsewhere the App Store shows the price in your currency before you buy.
  • Where to find it. The sticker on the Play screen, the "AI themes" section in Packs, the "Create with AI" tile when you choose a pack, or the Not Me! Plus section in Settings. The sticker reads "Your own AI theme" while your free theme is available, "Not Me! Plus" after that, and "Create a theme with AI" once you subscribe; if the Play screen shows no sticker, use one of the other entries.
  • You can close the studio. A theme keeps being created after you close the screen. When it is ready it appears in Packs and in the pack lists of the games it fits.
  • "Sign in to the App Store". The free theme is tied to your Apple Account, so the device must be signed in to the App Store (Settings → your name → Media & Purchases). Sign in, then tap the button in the studio again.
  • "That topic is a bit narrow" or "The AI couldn't turn this topic into a theme". The topic was too narrow to fill a theme, or it asked for something the content rules do not allow — sexual content, hate, real private people, instructions for harm. Rephrase it. A refused request does not create your free theme, but it uses one of its 3 attempts (see the next point).
  • "Your free try has been used". Each Apple Account gets one free theme and up to 3 attempts at it. A refused request, or one that failed after it was passed to the AI, uses an attempt; a request that failed before reaching the AI does not. Once the free theme is created or the 3 attempts are used, new themes need Not Me! Plus. If your free theme was created but never arrived — for example, the connection dropped — open the app again within 72 hours: it finishes the theme by itself, or tap "Finish that theme" in the studio.
  • "Free themes are paused" or "temporarily unavailable". The service is resting for a while — because of demand, a monthly limit or maintenance. Try again later; the app says when if it knows. Your games are not affected.
  • "Plus limit" or "Too many attempts in a row". Not Me! Plus allows up to 10 generations a day and 30 in any 30 days; a day starts at midnight UTC, and refused requests count too. The app tells you when you can create new themes. "Too many attempts in a row" means too many requests came from your internet address in a short time: take a short break and try again.
  • Restoring a subscription. On a new device or after reinstalling, tap "Restore purchases" on the Not Me! Plus screen or in Settings. If the app says "We couldn't confirm your subscription", tap "Restore purchases", and check that the subscription is active in Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  • Cancelling or changing plan. In the app, tap "Manage or change plan" in the Not Me! Plus section of Settings; or open the iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Not Me! Plus. Cancelling stops the next renewal; Plus works until the end of the paid period. Deleting the app does not cancel it. Themes you already made stay on your device.
  • Refunds. Payments are processed by Apple, and only Apple can issue a refund: reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • A generated card that should not be there? Tell us what the theme was about and what the card said, by e-mail.
  • Not Me! Plus and AI themes are not available in mainland China.

How to reach us

  • Email: InnoAppDesign@yandex.com — best for anything involving your name, your email, purchases, or details you do not want public.
  • Support form: https://forms.gle/f4DUfvcd97iUuqW68
  • Bug reports and feature requests: GitHub issues

All three are outside the app. The app has no contact button and no feedback screen, so write to us from your mail app or your browser. Whatever you type into an email, the form or a GitHub issue is what leaves your device; the form and GitHub are websites run by other companies, and the app sends us nothing about your message.

Nothing is attached automatically either. The app has no crash reporting and no diagnostics, so we only know what you describe in your message.

Write in English or Russian. Both are fine. The app is published by ИП Ахмадиев Альберт Маратович (individual entrepreneur Albert Maratovich Akhmadiev), Russia.

What to put in a report

The more of this you include, the faster we can reproduce the problem.

  1. Device model, for example iPhone 15 Pro or iPad Air.
  2. iOS version, from Settings > General > About on the device.
  3. App version. The current release is 1.2; the app's page in the App Store shows the version you have.
  4. App language you were playing in, Russian or English.
  5. Game mode and pack you were using.
  6. For AI themes: roughly when you tried, the message the app showed, and whether you have Not Me! Plus or were using the free theme.
  7. Steps to reproduce, in order, from opening the app to the moment it went wrong.
  8. What you expected to happen, and what happened instead.
  9. A screenshot or short screen recording, if it makes the problem clearer. Attach it to an email rather than posting it publicly.

Never send us your Apple Account password or payment card details. We will never ask for them.

Please keep private information out of GitHub

GitHub issues are public. Anyone can read them, and they stay readable after the issue is closed. Do not post your email address, phone number, full name, home address, screenshots of other apps, purchase receipts, or the contents of personal packs you wrote with your friends' names in them. If your report needs any of that, send it by email instead.

Privacy requests

The games collect nothing: no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no third-party SDK of any kind. Your settings, the last game modes you played, your own packs and your AI themes live on your device.

Only AI themes send data: your theme description goes to our server and to the AI provider to create the theme, together with Apple's signed purchase records and your IP address. Our server keeps keyed hashes of Apple transaction identifiers, a record of each recent generation for the fair-use limits (30 days), the finished theme for 72 hours, and short technical logs without your description. That is why the App Store privacy section for version 1.2 lists Other User Content, Purchase History, User ID and Product Interaction, all used only for app functionality and none for tracking. The full details are in the Privacy Policy.

To ask what we hold or to ask for deletion, email us. Our server does not know your name or email address, so tell us what you need and we will explain what we can find and delete. To erase everything on your device, delete the app. The rules for using the app and the subscription are in the Terms of Use.
